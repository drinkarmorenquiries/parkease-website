BUILT AROUND PEOPLE

Independence is the innovation.

For wheelchair users and drivers with limited mobility, a charging cable can become a physical barrier. ParkEase is designed to remove that step.

No heavy cables

Charging without manually lifting or plugging in a connector.

Stay in the vehicle

A proposed in-car charging experience with clear confirmation and stop controls.

Accessible by design

Parking bay layout, alignment guidance and fault recovery designed with disabled drivers.

THE SYSTEM

Wireless power. Practical engineering.

A ground-mounted primary coil transfers energy magnetically to a compatible receiver under the vehicle. Onboard electronics then regulate power through an approved vehicle charging interface.

Concept architecture — prototype validation and vehicle integration are still required.
